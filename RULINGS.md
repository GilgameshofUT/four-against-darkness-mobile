# Rulings & Interpretations (for review)

Where the rulebook (Four Against Darkness: Expanded Edition) was ambiguous, contradictory, or
needed adapting to a digital, pregenerated dungeon, a decision was made and recorded here.
Each item is marked **[REVIEW]** until you confirm or change it.

## Dice & checks
1. **Natural 1** auto-fails only Defense and Stealth rolls (as stated on p.96 and p.78). Attack rolls
   and Saves have no auto-fail on a 1 unless a specific rule says so. **[REVIEW]**
2. **XP rolls**: roll higher than current L; a 6 always succeeds (p.117). XP rolls do not explode. **[REVIEW]**
3. **Explosions** apply to every hero roll (Attack, Defense, Save, spellcasting, Stealth). Table rolls
   (treasure, reactions, morale, content) never explode. **[REVIEW]**
4. **Rerolls on failed rolls only.** Luck/amulets/traits are offered when a roll fails (to keep prompts
   light). The book allows a halfling to reroll any Attack/Defense/Save/Search/Treasure roll — rerolling a
   *success* is never useful, so this is equivalent. Treasure-table Luck rerolls are not yet offered. **[REVIEW]**

## Dungeon generation (digital adaptation)
5. **Pregenerated dungeon**: the whole 20×28 grid is filled at the start (cap 70 tiles) using the book's
   d66 tile shapes, rotated/mirrored/truncated to fit. Tile **content type (2d6)** is pre-rolled ("stocked");
   the specific Foe *type* is rolled live on entering, and the Foe's **L uses HCL at the moment of the
   encounter**, as the book requires. Treasure, traps, reactions and surprise are rolled live. **[REVIEW]**
6. Openings that lead into an already-placed tile connect to it (creating loops), rather than becoming
   dead ends. Openings at the grid edge are dead ends. **[REVIEW]**
7. Caverns and fungal grottoes are separate map regions (own 20×28 grid), reached by Secret Passages. **[REVIEW]**
8. **Special Feature (2d6 = 5)** in caverns/grottoes: the book's dungeon Special Feature table has no
   cavern/grotto equivalent beyond the Cavern Feature rule; we offer the Secret Passage option there. **[REVIEW]**

## Party & gold
9. **Party gold is pooled** during an adventure, capped at 200 gp × living heroes. The dwarf "Greedy"
   rule (each dwarf gets ≥1 gp) is therefore flavor only. 2+ dwarves still cannot Bribe. **[REVIEW]**
10. Between adventures, all gold goes to the **guild treasury** (shared). **[REVIEW]**
11. **Starting gear "choose slashing/crushing"**: light weapon = dagger/club, hand weapon = sword/mace,
    two-handed = greatsword/warhammer. A dwarf's slashing hand weapon is an Axe (for Axe Mastery). **[REVIEW]**
12. **Warrior bow kit** gives light armor + bow only (as written); the warrior fights unarmed (−2) in melee
    until another weapon is found. **[REVIEW]**

## Stealth
13. Stealth class bonuses follow each **class entry** where it conflicts with the summary box on p.78:
    halfling +L (class entry) vs +½L (summary); elf +1 (class entry) vs +½L (summary). Gnome: no bonus
    listed — treated as 0. **[REVIEW]**

## Combat
14. **Ranged weapons in rooms** can be used only on the first turn (before melee); afterwards heroes must
    spend a turn to draw a melee weapon or fight unarmed (−2). In corridors, rear-rank heroes may keep
    shooting. **[REVIEW]**
15. **Assigning foe attacks** is automated where the book is prescriptive (corridor front 2, hated class,
    wandering monsters target lowest Life), and asks the player where the book says "you decide". **[REVIEW]**
16. **Paladin "never breaks off"**: the Flee/Withdraw buttons are disabled while a paladin lives, unless all
    survivors are below ½ Life and healing is exhausted (the book's withdrawal clause). **[REVIEW]**
17. **Foe surprise when fleeing into an unexplored tile** is automatic, per p.98. **[REVIEW]**
18. **Trial of Champions vs a Minor Foe group**: their champion is one Minion 2 L higher with 1 Life. **[REVIEW]**
19. **Doppelganger, Hallucinogenic Horror** confusion: a confused hero may not attack that turn (they may
    still drink potions etc. outside combat). **[REVIEW]**
20. **Bear Form** damage is tracked separately; half (rounded down) carries over at the end of combat. **[REVIEW]**

## Spells & abilities
21. **Elves** cast only without a shield and in light/leafsteel/elven armor. Heavy armor blocks casting. **[REVIEW]**
22. **Gnome spellcasting bonus**: +L for its chosen illusionist spell and any spell with Illusion/Phantasm in
    its name; +1 otherwise. **[REVIEW]**
23. **Druid outdoor-only spells** (Lightning Strike, Entangle, Forest Pathway, Alter Weather) can be prepared
    but are disabled in dungeons (all environments in this book are underground). **[REVIEW]**
24. **Spell Burning** (wizard) is offered only when no prepared spells remain. **[REVIEW]**

## Text truncated in the book
25. Fiendish Magic Treasure #2 (ring of protection) text is cut off: "usable by any class except
    [barbarians]" — implemented as not usable by barbarians. **[REVIEW]**
26. Dungeon Boss "Chaos Lord" power 6 is unlabeled in the source text; implemented as Hellfire Blast. **[REVIEW]**

## Level cap
27. Heroes are capped at **L4** for now (Tier 1). Advanced/Expert skills (from other books) are not
    implemented. XP rolls are not offered to L4 heroes. **[REVIEW]**
